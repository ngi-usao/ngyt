Your Right to Opt-Out

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and similar state privacy laws, you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information. While NgiLeads Solutions does not sell your data to unrelated third parties for non-insurance marketing purposes, we do share your information with licensed insurance partners to fulfill our lead generation service.

How to Exercise Your Rights

If you wish to opt out of the sharing of your personal information, or if you wish to request the deletion of your data from our systems, you can do so by contacting us directly:

  • By Phone: Call us at (800) 365-6680 and state that you wish to opt out of data sharing.
  • By Email: Send a request to info@national-general-usa.com with the subject line "Privacy Opt-Out Request".
  • By Mail: Write to us at: NgiLeads Solutions, 7540 Austin St APT 2HR, Flushing, NY 11375.

What Happens When You Opt-Out

Please note that opting out of data sharing may limit our ability to connect you with insurance providers or process your request for an insurance quote. It will not affect the legal basis of any contracts already established between you and a third-party insurance provider.

Non-Discrimination

NgiLeads Solutions will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights. We will not deny you service, charge you different prices, or provide a different level of service solely because you exercised your rights under the CCPA or other privacy laws.
