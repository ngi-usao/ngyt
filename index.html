Good to know

Straight answers to common questions.

Are you the insurance company?
No. NgiLeads Solutions is an independent, licensed lead generation service. We don't write the policies ourselves — we connect you directly with licensed insurance providers and their representatives who can offer you tailored coverage.
Will the call buttons really dial?
Yes. Every "Call" button on this page dials (800) 365-6680 straight away on your mobile phone, and opens your default dialer on a computer. One tap, a real person in about 22 seconds.
What if I have tickets or an at‑fault accident?
Some of the companies we work with specialize in helping drivers with less-than-perfect records. Tell the agent your history up front, and they'll point you to the programs that treat you fairly — often well below what a big-name insurer quotes.
Does getting a quote affect my credit score?
No. The quick comparison only requires a soft check (which does not impact your score) to find your best rate. You can stop the process at any time with zero effect on your credit.
